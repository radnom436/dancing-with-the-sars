Scientific Processing

Δσ⁰(dB) = 10 · log₁₀(max(post, ε) / max(pre, ε))

Calculated in linear power ($\gamma^0$). Inundation causes specular scatter away from antenna, creating characteristic backscatter drops (< -3 dB).
